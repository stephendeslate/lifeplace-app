# Getting Support

The built-in support ticketing system lets you manage and respond to client inquiries directly within LifePlace. Access the support dashboard at `/support`.

## Dashboard Overview

At the top of the support page, a stats row summarizes your current workload:

- **Open Inquiries** — total unresolved tickets
- **Unassigned** — tickets not yet assigned to a staff member
- **Urgent Priority** — tickets flagged as urgent
- **Resolved Today** — tickets closed during the current day

> **Note:** The backend also tracks an "In Progress" ticket count, but this statistic is not currently displayed in the UI.

## Submitting and Viewing Inquiries

1. Navigate to the support page (`/support`).
2. Use the search bar to find existing inquiries by subject, client name, or email.
3. Apply filters to narrow results by status, category, priority, or assigned staff member.

| Filter | Options |
|--------|---------|
| Status | Open, Awaiting Response, Resolved, Archived |
| Category | Billing & Payments, Event Questions, Technical Issues, General Inquiry |
| Priority | Urgent, High, Normal, Low |
| Assignment | All, Unassigned |

## Working with an Inquiry

Click any inquiry to open its detail view. The main panel displays a chat-style thread showing client messages and admin replies in chronological order.

**Internal notes** are visible to staff only and appear with a yellow highlight and a lock icon. Use these to share context with your team without the client seeing the message.

## Updating Status and Priority

From the detail sidebar, you can:

1. Change the ticket **status** (for example, move from Open to Awaiting Response after replying).
2. Adjust the **priority** level to escalate or de-escalate the inquiry.
3. Reassign the ticket to a different staff member.

All status and priority changes are logged in the ticket history for audit purposes.
