# Quote Templates

Quote templates let you pre-configure standard pricing and terms so that new quotes start pre-populated rather than blank.

## Setting Up a Template

Navigate to **Settings > Commerce > Sales** to create and manage quote templates. Each template includes:

- **Default products and packages** -- the line items that will be pre-filled
- **Terms and conditions** -- standard contract language
- **Default tax rates** -- applicable tax percentages
- **Associated workflow templates** -- links the quote to a specific automation workflow
- **Validity period** -- how long quotes remain valid before expiring (default: 30 days)

## Applying a Template

When you apply a template to an event, a new **DRAFT** quote is automatically created with all of the template's products and pricing pre-populated. You can then adjust line items, pricing, or terms before sending the quote to the client.

Templates are especially useful when your venue offers standardized packages and you want to ensure consistent pricing across events.
