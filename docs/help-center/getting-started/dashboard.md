# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home base in LifePlace. It gives you a high-level view of what is happening across your venue's operations and provides shortcuts to common actions.

---

## What the Dashboard Shows

### Key Metrics

At the top of the Dashboard, you will see summary metrics:

- **Total Events** -- the number of events in the system
- **Revenue** -- total revenue recorded
- **Upcoming Events** -- events scheduled in the near future
- **Pending Tasks** -- tasks assigned to you or your team that are not yet complete

### Recent Activity

Below the metrics, the activity feed shows the latest updates across the platform:

- New events created
- Payment activity
- Client actions (booking requests, quote responses, contract signatures)
- Status changes on events

### Quick Actions

The Dashboard provides buttons to jump directly to common tasks:

- **Create a new event**
- **Add a client**
- **View upcoming tasks**

### Pipeline Overview

The pipeline section shows your events grouped by status:

- **Lead** -- initial inquiries and booking requests
- **Confirmed** -- events with signed contracts and confirmed dates
- **Completed** -- events that have taken place

This gives you a snapshot of your booking pipeline at a glance.

---

## Best Practices

Start each work session on the Dashboard to see what needs your attention. Check for:

- New pending tasks that have been assigned to you
- Recent client activity that may need a response
- Events approaching their dates that may need follow-up
- Pipeline balance -- are leads converting to confirmed events?
