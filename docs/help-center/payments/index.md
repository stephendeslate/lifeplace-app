# Payments & Invoices

LifePlace tracks all payments for your events, from initial deposits through final balances. Invoices are generated automatically when quotes are accepted, and payments can be recorded manually or processed online through Stripe or PayMongo.

## Articles

- [Recording and Tracking Payments](recording-and-tracking-payments.md) -- Create payment records, track statuses, and view payment details.
- [Invoices](invoices.md) -- How invoices are generated, what they contain, and how they track outstanding balances.
- [Stripe, PayMongo, and Receipts](stripe-and-receipts.md) -- Online payment processing via Stripe and PayMongo, and receipt generation.
- [Refunds and Disputes](refunds-and-disputes.md) -- Processing refunds, handling payment disputes, and managing evidence.
- [Payment Terms and Scheduling](payment-terms-and-scheduling.md) -- Downpayment rules, balance due dates, auto-cancel policies, and deposit settings.
