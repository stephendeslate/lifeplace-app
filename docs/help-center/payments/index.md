# Payments & Invoices

LifePlace tracks all payments for your events, from initial deposits through final balances. Invoices are generated automatically when quotes are accepted, and payments can be recorded manually or processed online through Stripe.

- [Recording and Tracking Payments](recording-and-tracking-payments.md) -- Create payment records, track statuses, and view payment details.
- [Invoices](invoices.md) -- How invoices are generated, what they contain, and how they track outstanding balances.
- [Stripe and Receipts](stripe-and-receipts.md) -- Online payment processing via Stripe and receipt generation.
