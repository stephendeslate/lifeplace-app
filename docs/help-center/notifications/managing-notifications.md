# Managing Notifications

Click the **bell icon** in the header to view your notification feed. Notifications keep you informed about important activity across the platform in real time.

## Notification Categories

Notifications are organized into categories based on the area of the platform they relate to:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| System | Account updates, security alerts |
| Event Management | New booking, event status change |
| Task Management | Task assigned, task due soon |
| Payment Processing | Payment received, payment overdue |
| Client Management | New client registered, client updated profile |
| Contract Management | Contract signed, contract expiring |
| Workflow Updates | Workflow stage advanced, automation triggered |
| Communication Updates | Email delivered, email bounced |
| Marketing & Promotions | Campaign updates |

## Notification Feed

Your notification feed displays all recent activity. You can interact with notifications in several ways:

- **Click through** — select any notification to jump directly to the related record (event, payment, client, etc.)
- **Mark as read** — click the indicator on a notification to mark it as read, or use **Mark All as Read** to clear the entire feed
- **Delete** — remove individual notifications you no longer need to see

Unread notifications are highlighted so you can quickly spot new activity.

## Workflow-Triggered Notifications

Workflows can automatically generate notifications when stages complete or specific conditions are met. For example, when a workflow advances past the contract-signing stage, the assigned staff member receives a notification prompting them to follow up. These automated notifications appear alongside all other notifications in your feed.

## Notification Preferences

Configure which notifications you receive and how they are delivered.

Navigate to **Settings > Account Management > Notifications** to access your preferences. From there you can:

- **Toggle categories on or off** — disable notifications for categories that are not relevant to your role
- **Disable per notification type** — turn off specific notification types within a category for finer control
- **Choose delivery channels** — select how you want to be notified for each category:
  - In-app (notification feed)
  - Email
  - SMS (where supported)
  - Push notifications
- **Quiet hours** — set a time window during which non-critical notifications are silenced (e.g., outside business hours)
- **Digest frequency** — choose how often digest summaries are sent (Immediate, Hourly, Daily, or Weekly) instead of receiving every notification individually
- **Marketing consent** — opt in or out of marketing and promotional notifications separately from operational notifications

## Push Notifications

Manage your registered devices for push notifications at **Settings > Account Management > Push Devices**.

From this screen you can:

- View all devices currently registered for push notifications
- Remove devices you no longer use or recognize
