# Products and Packages

Products and packages form the foundation of your venue's service catalog. Products represent individual services you offer, while packages bundle multiple products together into a single, streamlined offering for clients.

## Accessing Products and Packages

Navigate to **Settings > Commerce > Products & Packages** to manage your catalog.

## Products vs. Packages

A **product** is a standalone service such as a venue rental, catering, or AV equipment. A **package** groups several products together, often at a bundled rate, making it easier for clients to select a complete event solution in one step.

## Pricing Models

| Model | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Fixed | A single flat rate for the service |
| Hourly | Charged per hour of use |
| Tiered | Rate changes based on volume or duration thresholds |
| Custom | Manually quoted pricing for unique requests |

## Pricing Units

| Unit | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Per Event | One charge covers the entire event |
| Per Person | Charged based on guest count |
| Per Hour | Charged based on event duration |

## Categories

Products are organized into hierarchical categories. Each category has a sort order that determines its position on the rates page. Categories help clients browse your offerings logically and allow your team to keep the catalog well structured.

## Product Features

Each product supports several feature flags to control how it appears and behaves.

- **Featured** — highlighted prominently on the rates page
- **Highlighted** — given visual emphasis within its category
- **Approval required** — must be approved by an admin before confirming
- **Allow multiple** — enables clients to select more than one unit
- **Quantity limits** — maximum quantity a client can select (requires **allow_multiple** to be enabled first)

## Capacity and Duration

Configure duration constraints (minimum and maximum hours) and guest capacity using three fields: **minimum_guests**, **maximum_guests**, and **recommended_guests**. Products can also be marked as supporting multi-day events for retreats, festivals, or extended celebrations.

## Tax and Media

Each product has an **is_tax_inclusive** flag that indicates whether its listed price already includes tax. Tax is calculated using a single global default tax rate configured in the payments domain. Add gallery images to showcase each product or package visually. Products can also be associated with specific event types so they appear only for relevant bookings.
